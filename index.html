Walk with heat in mind.

Compare a shortest walking route with an option that weighs estimated heat exposure, shade and mapped cooling stops.

Demo data only. Scores are illustrative, not live weather or a medical/safety guarantee.

How the score works

Each street segment has a sample heat-risk value (0–1), estimated shade percentage, distance and an optional cooling-stop flag. The route finder ranks distance and a transparent distance-weighted heat/shade cost. These are sample values for demonstrating the method.
